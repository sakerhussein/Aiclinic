Limitsiz Clinic · voice assistant · step 4, the speech test

Booking by voice: recordings for the speech test

الحجز بالصوت · تسجيلات اختبار خدمات الكلام

40 short voice messages, the kind a patient sends on WhatsApp or the clinic's web chat to book, change or ask about an appointment. They decide which speech-to-text service understands Jordanian patients best. No real patients: everyone uses the made-up names and numbers on the cards.

Who

  • 8 to 10 people: clinic staff, family, friends. Not patients.
  • Mix men and women, young and older, and accents: Amman, the north, the south, rural or Bedouin, and someone who mixes in English.
  • Each person records 4 or 5 lines, so 40 to 50 recordings in all. Write who records what in the last column of the lists.
  • Each person gets a code, s01, s02…, on the speakers sheet. Codes, not names, go on the files.

Before recording

  1. Everyone signs the agreement, one copy each.
  2. Choose a private folder for the recordings that only your team can open. Not this project folder, and not a WhatsApp group.

Two kinds of line

  • Lines to read (R01 to R25): say the Arabic line in your own accent, as if sending a voice note. Small slips are fine; note them on the tracking sheet.
  • Say it your way (F01 to F15): read the situation, then say it in your own words, like a real patient. Afterwards, write down exactly what you said, or ask the checker to.
  • Lines marked key carry the details that matter most: day, time, doctor, service, name and phone digits.

Each recording

  • Use the phone's voice recorder app. Hold the phone the way you hold it for a WhatsApp voice note.
  • Each line names a place. Most are a quiet room; some are on purpose in a car, the street, a waiting room with the TV on, or a café.
  • Speak at your normal speed. Don't stop for mistakes.
  • Use only the names and phone numbers on the card.

After each recording

  • Name the file with the line and your code, for example R07-s03. A second take is R07-s03b.
  • Fill in its row in the tracking sheet, tracking.csv.
  • Copy the file to the private folder. On WhatsApp, send it as a document, not as a voice note.

What happens next

  • Each recording goes to three or four speech services, to compare them.
  • Someone who speaks Jordanian Arabic checks one transcript per recording against what was said: a few minutes each.
  • Two scores: overall word accuracy, and, separately, how often the key details come out right. A wrong time or digit matters far more than a wrong "و".
  • Then the voice for spoken replies: staff listen to samples and choose (last page).
  • Voice notes in the web chat, the staff form and WhatsApp are built only after this test.

Sign before recording · one copy per person

Agreement to take part in test recordings

I agree to record short test voice messages for Limitsiz Clinic's voice assistant. I understand that:

  • The messages are made up. I will use the names and numbers on the cards, and nothing about my own health or anyone else's.
  • My voice will be sent to three or four speech-to-text services, only to test them. These are companies outside Jordan, and each handles what it receives under its own terms.
  • The recordings and their text are used only for this test. Limitsiz deletes its copies when testing ends, or sooner if I ask.
  • I can withdraw at any time, and my recordings will then be deleted.

Later · when the voice samples are ready

Choosing the assistant's voice

For spoken replies, Limitsiz will send six short samples (A to F): the same reply read by different voices. Three to five staff listen on a phone and fill in one sheet each. A flat foreign accent would hurt trust, so if no voice sounds right, the assistant starts with written replies only.
