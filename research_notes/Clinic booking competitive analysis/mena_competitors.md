# MENA competitors for Limitsiz Clinic Booking

Research date: 6 November 2026. All prices are as shown on that date unless another date is given. Almost all feature and result claims come from vendors and were not independently verified.

Tool limits that shaped these notes: search returned few Arabic-language results; doctoruna.com refused the connection; the Vezeeta Jordan URLs tried (`/ar-jo`, `/en/doctor/dermatology/jordan`) returned 404; the Google Play page for Vezeeta For Doctors could not be read; GoodFirms returned 403. A 404 on a guessed URL does not prove Vezeeta has left Jordan.

## Which products are present in Jordan, KSA, UAE and Egypt?

### Takeaway
The confirmed Jordan field is thin: Eskadenia (local enterprise vendor), Altibbi (Jordan-founded telehealth with a clinic app), DoctorUna and Vezeeta (marketplaces, current Jordan status unconfirmed), BarmajTek (small local), plus low-cost regional entrants that claim Jordan (3yadtk, Clinit). The strongest WhatsApp AI products are in the Gulf, and none of them was found to name Jordan.

### Cited Findings

**Jordan**
- Eskadenia is a Jordanian vendor selling ESKA CareNet, a cloud clinic management and EMR system — [Eskadenia](https://eskadenia.com/eskadenia_software_launches_a_cloud_based_clinic_management_system)
- Eskadenia's named customer is Diva Skin & Dental Care Clinics, a dermatology and dental chain, which matches our first two target specialties — [Eskadenia](https://eskadenia.com/diva_care_clinics_successfully_launched_eska_carenet_powered_by_eskadenia_software)
- Altibbi started in Amman as an Arabic medical reference; from 2019 doctors could activate an e-clinic for patient files, appointments, bookings and health records — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altibbi)
- Vezeeta launched in Jordan in 2017 with over 400 subscribed doctors and offered clinics Vezeeta Care practice software — [Zawya, 2017](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/vezeetacom-launches-digital-health-solutions-in-jordan-pbppd9dh)
- An October 2022 article lists Vezeeta as active in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Lebanon, Jordan, Nigeria and Kenya — [Rasmal](https://www.rasmal.com/?p=3507)
- DoctorUna was described as available in the UAE, Egypt, Kuwait and Jordan, with a clinic system called UnaPractice (undated article) — [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/en-ae/entrepreneurs/paging-doctoruna-reem-haj-ali-wants-to-help-you-find-the/249627)
- BarmajTek builds Arabic-first software for clinics in Amman with JoFotara-ready billing; self-reported, founded 2024, 2–9 staff. Seen in a search listing only, as the page itself returned 403 — [GoodFirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/company/barmajtek)
- 3yadtk (عيادتك) claims 14 MENA countries including Jordan, with JOD invoicing to three decimals. It is operated by Limitless Royalty LLC in Florida — [3yadtk](https://3yadtisys.netlify.app/en)
- Clinit lists Jordan among its available countries, along with Bahrain, Kuwait, Morocco, Oman, Qatar, KSA and UAE — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1112301/Clinit)
- Jo iClinic offers remote consultations and appointment booking and supports clinics. The name suggests Jordan but the listing does not state a country; it has 1 rating — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6757619175)

**UAE and Qatar**

**Egypt**
- Vezeeta is the Egyptian-origin marketplace; in 2022 it reported subscriptions for more than 35,000 doctors, described as a first step toward software as a service — [Rasmal](https://www.rasmal.com/?p=3507)
- CliniDo, founded 2020 in Egypt, offers clinic booking, teleconsultation, home lab tests and home visits, and claims over 11,000 doctors and half a million patients — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/clinido-launches-a-new-service-allowing-patients-to-pay-in-installments-qroq2t9f)

- TabeebHub offers appointments, records, prescriptions, billing and a patient portal, with Arabic and English and a 14-day trial — [Product Hunt mirror](https://hunted.space/product/tabeebhub)

### Inferences
- A Jordanian dermatology clinic is most likely to meet Vezeeta or Altibbi as a demand channel, Eskadenia as the established local system, and spreadsheets or cheap regional tools at the small end. The likely-meeting ranking is my judgement, since no market share data was found.
- Marketplaces and clinic software are not direct substitutes. A clinic can list on Vezeeta and still need our product, so marketplaces could be a demand source to complement.
- JoFotara may become a purchase criterion if clinics fall under the mandate, and we have no invoicing today.

### Gaps
- Whether Vezeeta and DoctorUna are live for Jordanian clinics in late 2026 could not be confirmed. Both Jordan sources are old or undated.
- Tebcan: no reliable source found under this spelling or طبكان. It may be misspelled or not exist as a clinic product.
- Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib-type group systems: not researched successfully. No source was found on whether these chains sell software to outside clinics.
- Clinit's founding date and origin could not be settled. One search summary said Egypt-based and founded 2026, which was not checked against a primary page.
- No market share or adoption figures for clinic software in Jordan. "Medicus" and the Hakeem programme were not confirmed as relevant to private clinics.

### Inferences

## Which already offer WhatsApp booking or AI receptionists?

### Takeaway
Yes, several, and the closest rivals to our WhatsApp agent are Gulf products: Zavis, Mawidi, Mursalai and Basma. Zavis and Mawidi already have the staff hand-off that we have not built. None was found to name Jordan as a market.

### Cited Findings
- Zavis answers enquiries, offers open slots, books, confirms and reminds on WhatsApp, and also covers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, web chat, SMS and phone — [Zavis](https://www.zavis.ai/guides/clinic-front-desk-automation-uae)
- Zavis hands clinical questions, emergencies and uncertain cases to a person in under ten seconds with the conversation attached, and claims links to 52 clinic and hospital systems — [Zavis](https://www.zavis.ai/guides/clinic-front-desk-automation-uae)
- Zavis reminders go out 24 and 12 hours before a visit, and missed appointments trigger rebooking messages — [Zavis](https://www.zavis.ai/guides/clinic-front-desk-automation-uae)
- Mawidi covers WhatsApp, voice, SMS, email and social inboxes. Staff can take over a conversation and pause automated replies, and the voice agent can transfer calls — [Mawidi](https://www.mawidi.com)
- Mawidi takes deposits and no-show fees on its Professional plan and above — [Mawidi pricing](https://www.mawidi.com/pricing)
- Mursalai is described as a WhatsApp AI chatbot for medical clinics in KSA, UAE and Oman, handling bookings, reminders and follow-ups in Arabic and English — [Product Hunt mirror](https://www.hunted.space/product/mursalai)
- Basma (BrainSAIT) is pitched as an Arabic voice and WhatsApp booking agent for Saudi clinics, with insurance verification — [Basma](https://basma-app.pages.dev/)
- LetsBot offers a WhatsApp medical appointment API for KSA clinics — [LetsBot](https://letsbot.net/en/blogs/ksa-clinics-api-for-medical-scheduling-management.html)
- 3yadtk sends WhatsApp reminders with buttons, and has an AI scribe and assistant, but no AI booking agent is described — [3yadtk](https://3yadtisys.netlify.app/en)
- Okadoc's Fakeeh deployment used a WhatsApp bot for search and booking in 2022 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/corporate-news/fakeeh-university-hospital-dubai-silicon-oasis-digitalises-patient-experiences-with-okadoc-1.1653049876615)
- Freelancers on Khamsat sell WhatsApp booking bots built on n8n, from about $20 — [Khamsat](https://khamsat.com/ai-machine-learning/ai-agent-automation/4455757-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%B0%D9%83%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%AC%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7%D9%87-%D8%A8%D8%AC%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%84-%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%AF%D8%B1-n8n)
- SleekFlow reports WhatsApp Business API prices will rise on 1 October 2026, which affects any pass-through cost — [SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/ar/blog/salon-text-message-marketing-whatsapp)

### Inferences
- The hand-off inbox is table stakes among the leading AI reception products, so our missing hand-off inbox and staff chat replies are the clearest feature gaps.
- Levantine Arabic is an opening. Mawidi's public claim is about Gulf dialect, and no competitor found claims Jordanian dialect. This is an absence of evidence, not proof.
- Our own calendar is an edge against Zavis-type overlays, which sit alongside an EMR. In Jordan there may be no EMR to connect to at small clinics, though this was not measured.

### Gaps
- No independent test of any AI agent's booking success or dialect quality.
- Mursalai, Basma and LetsBot pricing and live customer counts were not found.
- No evidence of Eskadenia, Altibbi or Vezeeta offering an AI WhatsApp agent, but their pages were not exhaustively checked.
- Compliance claims (Saudi PDPL, Jordan's data protection law) were not verified for any vendor.

## What do clinics complain about or like in reviews?

### Takeaway
Clinic-side reviews are scarce. The clearest signal is doctor anger at Vezeeta's booking packages and no-shows; for the software vendors, only self-reported testimonials were found.

### Cited Findings
- Vezeeta For Doctors had a 3.5 rating from about 1.26K reviews, with reviewers calling new booking packages unfair, reporting trouble unsubscribing, and raising patient no-shows. Taken from a search result; the page itself could not be read — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drbridge.Doctors&hl=en)
- A 2021 App Store review said a clinic followed its own reservation system, so the Vezeeta booking was not honoured — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/vezeeta-doctors-pharmacy/id1010281314)
- Vezeeta's Trustpilot page has only 3 reviews and a 2.8 score. Complaints cover a failed video consultation with unreachable support (Nov 2025) and fees changing between visits (Aug 2023); praise covers easy booking with insurance (2016) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vezeeta.com)
- Altibbi for Doctors has 4.3 from 8 ratings. A February 2025 review says a doctor declined to join over security and trust concerns and found it complicated — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id915777710)
- Clinicy reports 85% fewer no-shows and SAR 2M a year recovered, and named clinics report no-shows falling from 35% to 6% and 50% to 13%. All vendor-provided, and one testimonial is labelled "Example" — [Clinicy](https://clinicy.com.sa)

### Inferences
- Pricing tied to bookings and hard cancellation are the sore points for marketplaces. A flat fee with easy exit is a possible message against them.
- No-shows are the recurring pain across both vendor marketing and doctor reviews, so reminder and confirmation results are likely to be the strongest proof point.
- Trust and data security can block doctors from adopting a platform, based on one review only.

### Gaps
- No independent reviews found for Eskadenia, Clinicy, Rekaz, Zavis, Mawidi, 3yadtk or any Jordanian product.
- No clinic forums or Arabic-language doctor discussions surfaced. Facebook groups for Jordanian doctors are a likely source but could not be searched, and direct interviews would fill this gap better.
- Sample sizes are very small, so these complaints show themes and not frequency.
