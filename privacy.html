Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Limitsiz Clinic is based in Amman, Jordan. You can contact us at management@limitsiz.si or on WhatsApp at 00962-795016514.

2. Information we collect

When you request a demo or contact us, we collect the details you submit: your name, clinic name, email address, phone number, and any message you write. We also collect basic technical information needed to keep the website working and secure.

3. How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, arrange demonstrations, and tell you about our services. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where it is stored

Contact and demo request details are stored in a secure database hosted on Supabase. Only authorized members of our team can access them.

5. Patient data

This website does not collect patient records. Clinics that use our product keep their patient data in their own database, and they control who can access it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep request details for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. Email management@limitsiz.si and we will respond.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
