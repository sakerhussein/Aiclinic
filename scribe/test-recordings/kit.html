Limitsiz Clinic · AI Scribe · step 1

Test recordings: kit for the recording day

الكاتب الذكي · تسجيلات الاختبار

24 role-played dermatology consultations, 5 to 10 minutes each. No real patients. They test two things: how well speech services understand Jordanian Arabic mixed with English medical words, and how good the AI's draft notes are.

Who

  • Doctors play themselves. Speak as you do with real patients: Arabic, with the English words you normally use.
  • A colleague or volunteer plays the patient. They read only the patient part of the card (in Arabic), then talk naturally. Change the patient's age or gender to suit them.
  • Mix it up: several doctors, men and women, different "patients" and accents.

Before the day

  1. A doctor checks every case. The plans are suggestions: change any medicine or dose you'd handle differently, and write the change on the card.
  2. Choose who records which case (the case list has a column for it).
  3. Everyone who will be recorded signs the agreement, one copy each.
  4. Choose a private folder for the recordings that only your team can open. Not this project folder, and not a WhatsApp group.

Set-up

  • A real examination room, door closed. Normal sounds such as the air conditioning are part of the test. Some cases add noise on purpose.
  • The device sits on the desk between doctor and patient, where it would be in a real visit. Each case says phone or laptop. Use a tablet instead of the laptop if that's what the clinic would use.
  • Use the device's normal voice recorder app.

Each recording

  • Read the card, then start recording. Don't read the card aloud.
  • Say the examination aloud: where, size, colour, spread. The Scribe only hears.
  • Say each medicine's name, strength, dose and how long, aloud.
  • Do the case's test points. They are small traps for the AI, such as changing your mind about a medicine.
  • Don't stop for mistakes. Real visits have them.
  • No real patients' names or details. The person playing the patient uses the card's story, not their own health.

After each recording

  • Name the file with the case number, for example case-07. A second take is case-07b.
  • Fill in its row in the tracking sheet, tracking.csv.
  • Copy the file to the private folder. On WhatsApp, send it as a document, not as a voice note, so the sound isn't compressed.
  • The doctor writes the "right" note on the template at the end, the same day if possible. Write what was actually said, not what the card says. Listen back if unsure.

Next: step 2

  • The recordings go to three or four speech services, to compare them.
  • For word accuracy we need an answer key: someone who speaks Jordanian Arabic corrects one transcript per recording, roughly half an hour each.
  • Medicines and doses are checked against the right notes.

Agreement to take part in test recordings

I agree to take part in role-played test recordings for Limitsiz Clinic's AI Scribe. I understand that:

  • The consultations are made up. I will not mention real patients or my own health.
  • My voice will be sent to three or four speech-to-text services, and the text to an AI service (Claude, by Anthropic), only to test them. These are companies outside Jordan, and each handles what it receives under its own terms.
  • The recordings and their text are used only to test the AI Scribe. Limitsiz deletes its copies when testing ends, or sooner if I ask.
  • I can withdraw at any time, and my recordings will then be deleted.

Acne: starting isotretinoin

Phone · quiet room · 7 to 10 min

للمريض: اقرأها قبل التسجيل، وبعدين احكي بشكل طبيعي

رهف، ٢٠ سنة، طالبة جامعة. وزنك ٦٠ كيلو.
الشكوى
حب شباب بالوجه والظهر من سنتين، وبيترك آثار.
علاجات سابقة
جل ديفرين (Differin) ٦ شهور، وحبوب دوكسيسايكلين (doxycycline) ٣ شهور السنة الماضية. ما نفعوا كتير.
أسئلتك للدكتور

For the doctor

Say aloud
Inflammatory papules and pustules on the cheeks, forehead and upper back; a few nodules on the jawline; some small depressed scars on the cheeks.
Assessment
Moderate to severe acne, not responding to adapalene and doxycycline.
Plan
Isotretinoin (Roaccutane) 20 mg once a day with food. Before starting: CBC, liver function, lipid profile and a pregnancy test. Two methods of contraception during treatment and for one month after. Lip balm and moisturiser. No waxing, laser or peeling during treatment.
Follow-up
1 month, with the blood results.

Test points: the draft note must get these right

  • Weight 60 kg; isotretinoin 20 mg once a day.
  • The four tests: CBC, liver function, lipids, pregnancy test.
  • Don't ask about allergies. The note must say allergies "not mentioned".
  • Differin and doxycycline belong in the history, not the plan.

للمريض: اقرأها قبل التسجيل، وبعدين احكي بشكل طبيعي

إذا سألك الدكتور

أسئلتك للدكتور

For the doctor

Say aloud
No hives at the moment; the photos show raised red wheals on the arms and trunk. Stroke the forearm lightly and say it rose into a line (dermographism).
Assessment
Chronic spontaneous urticaria, made worse by anti-inflammatory painkillers.
Plan
Cetirizine 10 mg, one tablet at night; if not controlled after one week, one in the morning and one at night. Avoid ibuprofen and aspirin; paracetamol is fine for headaches. Blood tests: CBC and TSH. Follow-up
4 weeks.

Test points: the draft note must get these right

  • Omalizumab (Xolair) is a later option, not prescribed.
  • Ibuprofen as a trigger in the history; "avoid ibuprofen and aspirin" in the plan.
  • The lips sometimes swell; no breathing problems.
