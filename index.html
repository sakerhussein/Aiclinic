05 — Security and privacy

Your patients' data belongs to your clinic.

Intelligence without limits, inside limits you set.

  • Your database

    Patient records are stored in your own local or cloud database, not in a third-party platform's storage.

  • Role-based access

    Staff see only what their role requires.

  • Doctor in the loop

    No clinical note or report is final until a licensed doctor approves it.

  • Encryption

    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

  • Clear data handling

    Patient data is not used to train AI models.

08 — FAQ

Questions clinics ask us.

Do I need to change my current software?

No. Limitsiz Clinic connects to the tools you already use. We start with what you have and move at your pace.

Will patients know they are talking to AI?

Yes. The Booking Agent and Receptionist identify themselves as your clinic's AI assistant, and urgent cases always go to your staff.

Can my doctors override the AI?

Always. Doctors review and approve every clinical output. The AI supports decisions. It does not make them.

Where is my data stored?

In your own database, either on your premises or in a cloud account you control.

How long does setup take?

Typically 1–2 days.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Patients can book and ask questions in Arabic and English.
