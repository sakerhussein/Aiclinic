Most clinic software does one job. Limitsiz Clinic runs a set of specialized AI agents that share information and hand tasks to each other. A booking becomes a reminder, a visit becomes a clinical note, and a payment becomes a financial record, without anyone re-typing the data.

Our Agents

Booking Agent (WhatsApp)

Patients book, reschedule, and cancel appointments by WhatsApp, any time of day. The agent checks your real availability and sends automatic reminders.

AI Receptionist

Answers common questions about services, prices, hours, and location. Routes urgent or complex calls to your staff with the context already summarized.

AI Scribe

Turns consultation notes into structured clinical documentation. The doctor reviews and approves every note before it is saved.

Medical Reports

Generates visit summaries and patient reports in seconds, ready for the doctor's review and signature.

Clinic Financials

Tracks daily revenue, payments, outstanding invoices, and service performance in one dashboard.

Clinical Assistant

Supports the doctor with reference information and checklists during care. The doctor always makes the final clinical decision.

How It Works

  1. 1. Connect

    Link your WhatsApp number, calendar, and existing systems. Setup is guided, and we handle the technical work.

  2. 2. Configure

    Add your services, staff schedules, pricing, and clinic tone of voice. The agents learn your workflow, not a generic one.

  3. 3. Orchestrate

    The agents work together around the clock. You see everything in one place and step in only where your judgment is needed.

A Day in the Clinic

  • 8:12 AM. A patient messages on WhatsApp asking for a check-up. The Booking Agent offers three slots and confirms the one she chooses.
  • 11:40 AM. A caller asks about pricing. The Receptionist answers and passes the call to staff only when a person is needed.
  • 2:05 PM. The doctor finishes a consultation. The Scribe drafts the note, and the doctor approves it in one click.
  • 6:00 PM. The Financials agent updates the day's revenue and flags two unpaid invoices for follow-up.

Your team did the same work. The difference is that nothing fell through the cracks.

Security and Privacy

Your patients' data belongs to your clinic.

Your database

Patient records are stored in your own local or cloud database, not in a third-party platform's storage.

Role-based access

Staff see only what their role requires.

Doctor in the loop

No clinical note or report is final until a licensed doctor approves it.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Clear data handling

Patient data is not used to train AI models.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to change my current software?

No. Limitsiz Clinic connects to the tools you already use. We start with what you have and move at your pace.

Will patients know they are talking to AI?

Yes. The Booking Agent and Receptionist identify themselves as your clinic's AI assistant, and urgent cases always go to your staff.

Can my doctors override the AI?

Always. Doctors review and approve every clinical output. The AI supports decisions. It does not make them.

Where is my data stored?

In your own database, either on your premises or in a cloud account you control.

How long does setup take?

Typically 1–2 days.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Patients can book and ask questions in Arabic and English.
